Who we are

We are a team of designers, developers, nutritionists, pediatricians, and thinkers. We consider ourselves big kids. We experiment and make digital apps that kids and parents love. Our goal is to make children more curious, intelligent, healthy, and, above all, happier.

Our values

We believe that happy children change the world. That learning and fun are the best we can offer them. With fun, learning gets much tastier and better. Let's put smiles and good habits in our children's lives.
